# Adjacent topics:
- Technofeudalism





I feel that contemporary life is marked by this notion of Technofeudalism that our standard of living is ever improving and consequently the resources in both material and labor are expanding. As we strive for a higher and higher standard of living, it demands that we earn more and more. It's not quite  a debt peonage system, but even if you are not in debt, but feel an obligation to consume and acquire more with no sense that you can rest the behavior is the same of continually being obligated to work. It is a sort of psychological debt and this is where Technofeudalism comes in. Because nothing is owned, though you are not in debt, anything you "have" is only had temporarily. So if you were to exit the system, you have no obligation to another person, however attachment to a standard of living demands that you're earning potential must be ever increasing. 



Forever temporary permission to use something in exchange for the temporary buying power afforded to you by your contributions.
