I spent a magical summer in LA and was introduced to the website *Revival Houses*. It's a great project that brings together all of the movie listings across LA. Aggregating movie listings is a preservation of sanity as the alternative is trolling the many cinema websites for interesting movies at convenient times.

I was inspired by *Revival Houses* to provide a similar service for San Francisco movie goers.
